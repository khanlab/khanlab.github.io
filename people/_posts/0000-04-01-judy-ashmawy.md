### About

Judy Ashmawy is a master’s student in Biomedical Engineering under the supervision of Dr. Ali Khan. She graduated in July 2026 from the Systems and Biomedical Engineering program at Cairo University. Her undergraduate research focused on computer vision and deep learning, especially shortcut learning in medical imaging. She now works on text-promptable models for 3D brain MRI, with applications in epilepsy surgery.
